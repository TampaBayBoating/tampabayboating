Manatee slow zones, in plain English

Where Tampa Bay slows down for manatees, what “idle” and “slow” really mean, and the dates that matter — checked against FWC’s official rules.

The surprise: most Tampa Bay slow zones are on all year — not just in winter. The seasonal dates are the exceptions (they’re marked on every card below), and at King’s Bay the spring sanctuaries close to boats completely Nov 15 – Mar 31. And one rule beats everything on this page: the posted sign on the water wins.

Hillsborough River — downtown Tampa

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Kennedy Blvd through downtown is idle speed, and so are the Seddon, Garrison, Sparkman and Ybor channels. Above Platt Street the river goes slow Nov 15 – Mar 15, and idle above Columbus Drive in that same season.

Alafia River

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed the whole river, all year. The marked channel allows 25 mph — and one posted stretch runs 20 mph on Sundays and holidays, so read the signs as you go.

Little Manatee River

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed year-round. The creeks around Cockroach Bay are idle speed.

Big Bend / TECO — Apollo Beach

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

The power plant’s warm-water outflow is idle speed, and it’s the rule all year — posted hardest in winter, when hundreds of manatees pack the canal next to the viewing center. Slow down and enjoy the show.

Tampa Bypass Canal & McKay Bay

IDLE / SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Idle at the canal mouth, slow speed inside the canal; McKay Bay is idle. Year-round.

Pinellas side of Tampa Bay

25 MPH MAX · YEAR-ROUND

Most of the open bay on the Pinellas side caps at 25 mph year-round. The shallows near shore go slow speed Nov 15 – Mar 31.

Old Tampa Bay — Safety Harbor

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Everything north of the Courtney Campbell Causeway is slow speed, all year.

Weedon Island

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed around the island; 25 mph allowed in the marked channel.

St. Pete waterfront

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Idle: Bayboro Harbor, Coffee Pot Bayou, John’s Pass and the city canals. Slow: Clam Bayou and Frenchman’s Creek. Around Fort De Soto, follow the posted slow and idle areas.

Clearwater & Boca Ciega Bay

POSTED · VARIES

A patchwork of posted slow and idle areas around Clearwater Beach, the Memorial Causeway and Sand Key — this stretch isn’t one simple rule. The signs run it; slow down when you see them.

Manatee River

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed year-round, with 25 mph allowed in the marked channels (20 in the Portosueno and Tropic Isles channels). The upper river, above the Fort Hamer area, is idle.

Terra Ceia Bay

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed across the aquatic preserve; 25 mph in the marked channels.

Braden River

SLOW / IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Slow south of the Manatee Avenue bridge — idle above it. Year-round.

Sarasota Bay — Ringling / Bird Key

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

The island area by the Ringling Causeway and Bird Key is idle speed, year-round.

Roberts Bay, Little Sarasota Bay, Blackburn Bay

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed in the bays and bayous (Lyons, Dona and Roberts). The only places 30 mph is fine are Big Sarasota Pass and the Intracoastal itself.

Midnight Pass & the island canals

IDLE / SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

The Midnight Pass remnant is idle; the Siesta and island canals run idle or slow depending on the stretch — signs decide.

Anclote River — Tarpon Springs

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed year-round; idle by the marinas and farther upriver.

Cotee River — Port Richey / New Port Richey

SLOW · YEAR-ROUND

Slow speed on the Pithlachascotee (everybody calls it the Cotee); idle farther inland.

Weeki Wachee River

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Idle speed the whole state-park reach — the run up to the spring is all idle, all year. The Mud River next door is idle too.

Bayport — Hernando Beach

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Idle speed at Bayport and Linda Pedersen Park.

King’s Bay & Crystal River

SLOW/IDLE YEAR-ROUND · SANCTUARIES CLOSED NOV 15 – MAR 31

Slow and idle zones all year — and in winter the spring sanctuaries (Three Sisters Springs and more) close to all boats, period. That’s when hundreds of manatees crowd the springs. See them from the boardwalks and the tour boats that know the boundaries.

Homosassa River

IDLE · YEAR-ROUND

Idle speed year-round in the river’s manatee zone.

What the speeds actually mean

IDLE

Idle Speed No Wake — the slowest speed that still lets you steer. Boat sits flat, zero wake behind you.

SLOW

Slow Speed Minimum Wake — fully off plane, bow down, smallest wake you can make. If you’re plowing a bow wave, you’re not slow.

25 / 30

Numbered zones — only where the rule or the signs allow it, usually the marked channel or the open bay. Step out of the channel, slow back down.

The ticket

Speeding in a manatee zone is a $100 civil fine under Florida law (§379.2431), plus possible court costs — enforced by FWC, sheriff marine units and the Coast Guard. Jet skis play by the same zones.

Three things people get wrong

The sign wins. A posted sign on the water beats this page, any app, and anybody’s memory — including ours. Signs change after storms and dredging.

No manatee in sight doesn’t matter. The speed is the law whether or not you see one — the zones protect the habitat, not just the animal in front of you.

It’s not one statewide rule. Florida wrote these county by county, river by river. Tampa Bay’s map is mostly year-round; the calendar dates on this page are the exceptions, not the rule.
