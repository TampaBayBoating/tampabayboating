December Boat Parades Around Tampa Bay

Every December lighted boat parade around the bay, night by night — where it runs, when it starts, and where to watch. Full details, entry info and any last-minute changes live on our Events calendar.

5–10pm

Snow Place Like Tarpon Springs & Boat Parade

Historic Downtown Tarpon Springs, E Tarpon Ave & Pinellas Ave. Illuminated boat parade through the Anclote River and the bayous, plus a downtown street festival with snow, a tree lighting and live entertainment. Santa arrives by boat in Spring Bayou about 8:30pm.

6pm

Hillsborough River Holiday Boat Parade (26th Annual)

Lowry Park to Sulphur Springs, Hillsborough River. Free community lighted boat parade — kayaks to powerboats — hosted by Friends of the Hillsborough River and the Tampa Bay Sierra Club. Get on the water 5:00–5:30pm; the parade runs 6–7pm, paddlers first.

4–7pm

42nd Annual Alafia Lighted Boat Parade

Riverview Park & Civic Center, 11020 Park Dr, Riverview. The 42nd annual lighted boat parade on the Alafia River — Santa, a chili cook-off and live music at the park.

6–9pm

Dunedin Holiday Boat Parade

Marker 1 Marina to Dunedin Marina, Dunedin. Decorated, illuminated boats parade along the Intracoastal to Dunedin Marina for the city's returning holiday boat parade.

6pm

Venice Christmas Boat Parade

Intracoastal Waterway, Venice — Albee Road Bridge to Circus Bridge. Decorated boats run south from Nokomis toward the Circus Bridge, with free viewing along the jetties and waterfront.

6–9pm

South Pasadena Holiday Light Boat Parade

MarineMax St. Petersburg, 6810 Gulfport Blvd S. Free lighted boat parade hosted by MarineMax — decorated boats cruising past South Pasadena, with a shore viewing party at Barbara Gilberg Park.

6pm start

52nd Annual Clearwater Holiday Lighted Boat Parade

Clearwater Yacht Club — Mandalay Channel to Clearwater Beach Marina. The 52nd annual decorated-boat procession; free entry for all vessels. Captain's meeting Thu Dec 10, 6pm at the yacht club.

Start time not yet published

Madeira Beach Festival of Lights Boat Parade (60th)

Madeira Beach waterfront / ROC Park. The 60th annual — one of Tampa Bay's longest-running lighted boat parades, hosted by the City of Madeira Beach.

Gather ~5:30pm · steps off 6:00pm

Boca Ciega Yacht Club Christmas Boat Parade (40th)

Boca Ciega Bay, Gulfport. The yacht club's 40th annual Christmas boat parade, paired with the Operation Santa toy drive — skippers donate new, unwrapped toys instead of an entry fee.

6:30pm

Downtown St. Pete Lighted Christmas Boat Parade

Downtown St. Petersburg waterfront (Municipal Marina area). Downtown St. Pete's own lighted Christmas boat parade along the waterfront.

6pm

Apollo Beach Lighted Boat Parade

Tampa Sailing Squadron, 1250 Apollo Beach Blvd. Lighted boat parade hosted by Tampa Sailing Squadron, with a chili cook-off and Christmas carolers at the launch site. Skippers: mandatory captain's meeting at the clubhouse on parade morning.

6pm

Manatee River Holiday Boat Parade

Manatee River, Bradenton — starts at Shaw Point (boats stage 5:30pm). Lighted boats parade past the Bradenton Yacht Club, Riverhouse and Pier 22. Public viewing from the Green Bridge and Palmetto Pier.

Start time not yet published

Sarasota Holiday Boat Parade of Lights

Sarasota Bay, Bayfront Park / Marina Jack. Lighted boat parade benefiting Suncoast Charities for Children.

6pm

St. Pete Beach Holiday Lighted Boat Parade

Boca Ciega Bay, St. Pete Beach. Lighted boat parade with cash prizes for the best-decorated boats — boaters wanted. Viewing party and concert at Horan Park, 7701 Boca Ciega Dr.

6:15pm

Tampa Riverwalk Holiday Lighted Boat Parade

Hillsborough River / Tampa Riverwalk. Tampa's largest lighted boat parade, cruising past Sparkman Wharf, Harbour Island and the Convention Center. Boat registration: (813) 221-1539.

Start time not yet published

Treasure Island Holiday Lighted Boat Parade

Treasure Island — Isle of Palms to John's Pass. Decorated-boat procession around Treasure Island's isles, finishing at John's Pass.

7pm

Indian Rocks Beach Christmas Boat Parade

Intracoastal Waterway, Indian Rocks Beach. No-registration lighted boat parade — bring a decorated boat and join the procession. Free.
