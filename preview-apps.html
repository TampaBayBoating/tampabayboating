The big apps charge $40 to $170 a year for charts, weather and fishing forecasts — and none of them know Tampa Bay. Here’s what they charge, and the local version of it you get here for $0.

Add it up

Stack just three of these — charts, weather planning and fishing — and you’re at roughly $230 a year, for national data that has never heard of Beer Can Island. The Tampa Bay layer is free here, because local sponsors pay for it — not you.

Today’s cheapest fuel

Non-ethanol and diesel prices, checked store by store every morning around Tampa Bay. Boaters plan their fuel stop around it. No national app sells this at any price — it has to be local, and it has to be checked.

Every event on the water

Boat parades, raft-ups, fishing tournaments, waterfront festivals — one calendar, free to browse and free to list your own event. Sponsors keep it that way.

Local rules, plain English

Manatee slow-speed zones and when they switch on, bridge heights, tricky passes, what’s legal to keep. The stuff that costs Tampa Bay boaters tickets and tow bills — explained like a neighbor, from the official rules.

Local people answering

This site is run by David and Nancy, two Tampa Bay boaters. When something here is wrong, a local fixes it — not a support ticket on another continent.

The honest part

We are not a chartplotter. Use real charts for navigation — Navionics earns its money there, and no website (including this one) should steer your boat. Anyone who tells you different is selling you something.

Towing membership is still worth it. One tow home without Sea Tow or BoatUS can run $600 or more. Buy the membership; use us for everything around it.

Our weather comes from NOAA and the National Weather Service — the same source the paid apps repackage. We translate it into a plain verdict for Tampa Bay instead of charging you to read it.

So how is all this free? Local marine businesses sponsor the site so boaters never pay. That’s the whole deal — no subscriptions, no paywall, no app to install.
